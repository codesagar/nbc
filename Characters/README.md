## Rules that travel with every selection

- **N35 is excluded from anatomy and motion endpoints.** Its folded-ear portrait has unresolved provenance. Do not redefine Neet's ears from it.
- **B08 is styling-only.** Its editing provenance is unverified; it cannot establish anatomy, eye size, symmetry or canonical color.
- **B25 remains unavailable.** A catalog mention is not an image, and it cannot be selected unless a genuine resolved source is supplied and reviewed.
- **B30 is supplemental resting evidence.** It establishes no standing proportions. B22 and B30 depict the same exposure; the active library keeps B30 and archives B22's other encoding.
- **An exposure counts once.** B04/B24 are also one photograph in different encodings; the active library keeps B04. Extra file containers do not add independent corroboration.

N35, B08 and alternate encodings are recoverable historical sources, not current anatomy references. The archive member paths and complete policies for these records remain in reference-policy.json. Photographs support visible regions only, with no calibrated dimensions, exact breed ancestry, weight, fixed color palette, motion timing or psychological state. Named photo collections support visual correspondence; original acquisition/editing provenance and owner approval remain independently unproved.

[Generated pose studies](../Assets/README.md) support acting and storyboard exploration. They never override original photographed anatomy. Historical “approved,” “canonical” and “installed” labels do not constitute a new creative approval.

## Source distinctions and unresolved choices

The physical notes below were condensed from original visual specifications and checked against the current original-photo views. Personality references originally targeted desktop avatars; retain their useful mannerisms as broad guidance rather than a mandatory scene checklist. Narrative guidance has another role: it supplies voice, household relationships and story behavior. A photograph cannot resolve a disagreement between those written character interpretations.

Neet's narrative voice emphasizes fearless overreaction, while his personality reference emphasizes quiet comfort and slightly timid behavior with cute “General Neet” seriousness. Choose the interpretation for the story being adapted and record that choice; neither was erased. Older weight/breed descriptions and a later “medium-to-large” description conflict, so artwork follows the photos and no new measurements are claimed. Neet's smooth short coat and tiny tail nub also conflict with haircut incidents and “helicoptering tail” wording in prose; adapt those incidents deliberately if used.

Full original specifications, personality documents, per-image inspection records and catalogs are preserved in the recovery archive. This is an editorial working selection, with no new canon or publication approval.
