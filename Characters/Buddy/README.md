## Personality and story voice

The personality reference describes a sweet, gentle, affectionate, easygoing dog who loves comfort and becomes focused and energetic around balls. His scruffy “Einstein” appearance contrasts with a proper gentleman sit; a tie or bow tie is occasional styling, not a permanent costume. Squeaky toys are a secondary written interest. Useful acting cues include coupled head/upper-body lean, chin near the ground, hind-leg reaching for attention, a natural yawn, belly-up bent paws and a compact bed curl. Photographs supply the visible poses; prose supplies motives and personality.

Narrative guidance gives Buddy dry, understated observations, good sense, occasional meekness and bravery despite acknowledged fear. Give him his own desire, choice and consequence. His relationships with both humans are independent; he should not become a passive commentator on their partnership. Affectionate exasperation with Neet's overdrive can support the comedy without taking away Buddy's agency.
