# Stories

Start here to find and read the collection. Each linked file opens with one selected working narrative. Where a story has a substantial competing draft, its **Meaningful alternative** section keeps the complete alternative collapsed below the primary; shortened variants use a concise passage/omission note. Provenance comes last.

The [expanded commission](../Productions/Library-Refinement/expanded-commission.md) is complete: all 70 primary stories and their meaningful alternatives received the second comedy pass. The edition contains **241 unique contextual illustrations**, with at least two in every primary and separate coverage where alternatives warrant it. Every selected picture passed independent native-resolution review; every complete illustrated story passed a fresh rendered reading. The [production record](../Productions/Library-Refinement/progress.json) links the evidence. Style and human likeness remain provisional, owner approval **unapproved**, and the [browser edition is published](https://codesagar.github.io/nbc/). [Open the clean browser reader](../Reader/index.html). Editing, sharing and publishing instructions are in [the reader guide](../Studio/Reader.md).

The **S###** prefix is a stable editorial shelf ID, not a historical chapter number or verified timeline. Keep it when renaming a title; use the next unused ID for a new story. All working files use `S###-lowercase-hyphenated-title.md`. New stories belong in this folder and receive one entry here. No separate story catalog needs synchronizing.

This library has **70 distinct chapter/story files**. The first group follows the manuscript’s source order while closing the *file-number* gap: its Chapter 7 is absent, so S007 is the supplied Monkey Menace (source Chapter 8). The household group uses source chapter labels as a browsing aid, keeps colliding but unrelated chapters separate, and places linked festival continuations together. The final group collects unnumbered episodes and independent outings. These are editorial shelves, not a claim that all drafts form a single consistent chronological manuscript.

Reconciliation: the joined Visarjan/Invisible Mentor document is split at its explicit second chapter heading; Chapter 10’s reversed fragment and Chapter 34’s duplicate add no new text and are represented once with reunited provenance. Two formatting variants merge into their manuscript stories; the aquarium and farewell abridgements stay within their story files. Smoky Mountain and apartment one-dog drafts, plus Houston’s expanded dinner version, remain in-file alternatives. The Piedmont Park wedding and the India wedding recalled by the tunnel story remain separate events of unresolved relationship.

Working selection does **not** mean owner-approved canon. Historical weight, fur, tail, breed, rank arithmetic, harsh reactions, food/care depictions and changing household voices remain draft choices. Use [character guidance](../Characters/README.md) for physical likeness and new production decisions. The farewell already reaches the U.S. before subsequent journey chapters; that source timeline is still unresolved. First-person diary voices and the one-dog alternative’s female pronouns are preserved. The consistent proper-name spelling Neet replaces the source spelling Neat in reading copies.

Find originals through the provenance footer and [Studio recovery guidance](../Studio/Recovery.md). Historical acquisition names are only recovery identifiers.
