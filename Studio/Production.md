# Producing an episode

Read the complete [working story](../Stories/README.md), meaningful alternatives, [writing guidance](Writing.md), [open questions](Open-Questions.md), character guides and intended-use photo policy. Copy [episode-template.json](episode-template.json) into one actual production folder, such as `Productions/E001-houston/episode.json`. Episode IDs are independent of historical chapters. The record holds current stage, next action, findings, sources, references, approval and publication once; keep creative reasoning in the script/brief rather than duplicate trackers.

| Stage | Required visible work and inspection |
|---|---|
| Story | Develop each dog's want, obstacle, attempts, complication, choice, consequence and earned payoff. Justify every beat and panel; choose length from the story. |
| Character model | Render both dogs together and in front, side, three-quarter and relevant action/rest views beside eligible photographs. Construction ratios are provisional, not measurements from uncalibrated photos. |
| Drawn storyboard | Actually draw the complete sequence, room layout, movement and lettering space. Show continuous spine, ribcage, pelvis, limb roots, support and occlusion. A prose/ASCII plan cannot substitute. |
| Panel comparison | Open every full panel and a comparison board beside model/photos. Check body connectivity, weight-bearing, limb ownership, scale, perspective, coat and grooming. Buddy needs a continuous long torso, neck and real leg clearance; Neet needs fine legs, large ears and a nub. Reject any material drift and reinspect after correction. |
| Complete reading | Inspect the entire lettered export in sequence at intended reading size for cause/effect, gag timing, dialogue order, emotional payoff, continuity and open findings. |

Generate finished panels after the developed story, model and drawn storyboard pass inspection. A single finished sheet cannot replace those stages. Passing file checks or reviewer agreement establishes no visual/story clearance. Record the actual artifact and concrete observations for each stage; material faults keep the episode `needs_rework`. Owner-reported faults reopen that status until corrected work is inspected.

Use meaningful version names: `script-v002.md`, `model-v001.png`, `p01-panel03-art-v002.png`, `reading-v001.pdf`. Keep editable composition, current prompts and parent artwork for each generation. Record exact input paths/hashes, roles, intended uses, full effective photo policies and exposure groups, tool arguments and visible settings. Unexposed model/seed stays unknown. Prompts do not guarantee regeneration; retain actual outputs. Original photos remain the likeness authority.

Keep one `episode.json` updated after each stage, including the next action. Freeze versioned delivered sources and exports within `delivery-v001/`, with a file/hash list, dimensions, format, sources and reviewed version. Preserve older delivered editions. Review and owner approval are separate; record absent approval as unapproved and publication as not_published. External release needs its own authorization.

The failed Night Shift experiment is recoverable in [Recovery](Recovery.md). Its weak story, disconnected resting anatomy, proportion drift, skipped model/storyboard and false clearance are diagnostic warnings, not creative precedent.
